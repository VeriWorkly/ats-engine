The 2026-10-22 full-repo audit's 64 open findings are fixed. Each fix has a test that failed before it, and the hidden-text and HTML changes were checked on real files: 38 PDFs and Word documents made with Chrome, Word and published resume themes (banners, sidebars, photos, icons, text boxes, watermarks, shaded tables) read exactly as in 0.20.0 with no hidden text, real covers are found, and 38 real job postings and careers pages read word for word as before. English resumes read as in 0.20.0: on 1,089 runs (every English fixture, 300 generated resumes, the private graded set and review cases, alone and with postings), no score, matched keyword or requirement status changes; requirement evidence now quotes a line with its column gap as one space. The bench lists no miss, in every language and format.

- **Breaking, hidden text:** a structured resume's invisible characters are counted on its fields as given, so tag characters in a JSON Resume are flagged as they are in text. A Word file's headers, footers and embedded pages are read only where a section or the body places them; its alternate content is measured as Word shows it; text hidden by a character or paragraph style, white or two-point text, or text squashed to a sliver is hidden. A PDF's text in a layer the file turns off is hidden, as is the part of a line a box drawn over it covers, and only that part, wherever the box sits (a box over half a bullet hid the whole bullet and its visible keywords); ruled tables are counted on a crowded page; a soft mask is checked within a fixed budget (24,000 glyphs under 24,000 paints: 15 s before, under 2 s now). An HTML resume's text hidden by a style sheet class or written near-white on no background is hidden, unless another of its classes shows it on some screen or in some state (utility classes such as "hidden lg:flex", a menu opened on hover) or it sits in the page's navigation or footer, which are a site's, not a resume's; and a self-closed hiding tag ("<span hidden/>") hides what follows, as a browser reads it. Hidden lines are left out of the job match even below the hidden-text rule's threshold (three hidden keywords no longer lift a match from 25 to 100).
- **Breaking, parsing:** role headers written as bullets ("- Senior Engineer, Globex Corp — Jan 2021 – Present") are roles when no other line is dated; several titles under one employer's line each keep it; ordinal days ("1st Jan 2020"), an arrow ("May 2019 → Present") and two-digit or season dates in structured input are read; LinkedIn's certification rows (name, issuer, "Issued … · Expires …") are one row, "PMP, CSM, CISSP" three, and an inactive one is "partial" against a requirement; a lowercase description is never joined onto its dated header; "Work Experiences", "Career History" and "Positions Held" are experience headings, and "History Teacher" is a title; phone numbers printed with en dashes are read; a role's span counts no month after today; links keep their own brackets and commas; an education entry's dates on a line of their own end it. A document naming another version of the structured format ("ats-resume@2") is an `AtsInputError`, not text.
- **Breaking, languages and policy:** an English resume with no bullets is weighed whole, so place names ("Des Moines", "Les Schwab") no longer read it as French; "a level 2 support role", "computer literate", "sales promotion" and "Certificate of Sponsorship" ask for no degree; a language row stating two levels reads the higher. A policy is refused, with the path, when a years or language pattern has no capturing group, a synonym's key has a space, or a region pack's rule id is one or two letters off a real one ("did you mean …"); other unknown rule ids are still ignored. Language names in a pack match in any case, seniority level names too. The fingerprint now covers the detection words of the packs a resume is read in. `check` no longer freezes a host's own unparsed policy.
- **Breaking, matching and scoring:** "AWS or Google Cloud" is two alternatives; a posting's structured qualifications and responsibilities are read under their own headings; requirement lines copied into a resume fail the copied-posting check; the words of a link's path and of the contact line are no keywords; short tools ("dbt", "git", "npm") are judged; a product named after a tool the policy knows ("Apache Pulsar") stays; a list repeating one term inside Experience is stuffing, a role's own "Technologies:" line is not; a twice-escaped posting is unescaped; "Resume.final" is advised as a draft. The report says when a resume was cut at 50,000 characters or a posting's requirements past the 25th were not judged, and gains `keywordCounts` (`matched`, `missing`), which a restricted report's counts use past twelve.
- **Breaking, AI:** `repairParse` reports a reply that fills no field the parse left empty, because it gave none or only values not in the resume or already parsed, as an `AtsAiError` with the new code `unusable`, instead of returning the parse unchanged as repaired. It is not retried (asked again, a model rarely finds more, and each attempt is billed) and carries `usage`, `attempts` and the dropped values in `rejected`. `runAiEval` reports it as `unusableRate`.
- **AI and CLI:** the `--ai` notice promises to hide only the contact details the parser read; `convertResume` drops an entry whose every identity value was invented; a suggestion's opening claim is checked unless it is an instruction verb; repair keeps a month only where the document writes it ("2019 - 2022" stays 48 months); an email glued to a dash is grounded; grounding thousands of values is fast; an HTTP 200 reply that is a JSON list is not retried; a UTF-8 file with a byte-order mark is never read as Windows-1252; the reader's child process reports the reader's own error and answers a malformed request.
- **Development:** `npm test` never runs the paid live eval (`npm run eval:live` has its own config), and `npm run test:private` runs the private-policy tests before a release (CONTRIBUTING). The GitHub Action stops the engine and every process it started at its time limit, and its README says to pin a `version` or a commit.
- The default policy's fingerprint is `ats-v2+6e295cb1` in this release. Bundles (gzip): `.` 115.2 KB, `./locales` 92.4 KB, `./ai` 81.1 KB, `./node` 25.7 KB, `./job` 4.5 KB.
