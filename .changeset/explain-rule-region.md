`explain_rule` takes an optional `region` and explains a rule as that country weighs it, as `check_resume` scores it. Without one it gave the published weights only: a photo showed 2 points where a US resume is scored on 6. A rule a region turns off (a photo in Germany) answers that it is not scored there. Without `region`, the answer is unchanged.
