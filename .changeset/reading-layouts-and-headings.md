**Breaking:** more page layouts, headings and role headers are read right, so recovered fields and scores change for the same input.

- **A heading in the left gutter**, printed level with the first line of its section (a moderncv-style page: "EXPERIENCE⇥Senior Software Engineer, Acme Corp⇥Jan 2020 - Present"), opens its section, and the rest of the line is that section's first line. The heading was read as part of the role ("Senior Software Engineer" at "EXPERIENCE, Acme Corp"), the degree beside "EDUCATION" as a job, the skills were lost, and the structure rules found no headings. Inside Skills, a languages or certifications heading in the gutter is still a category of skills, as one with a colon is.
- **A PDF's running header and footer** are read once, before the text and after it, as a DOCX's are, and its page numbers ("Page 1 of 2") not at all. A line is one when it is among the top or bottom two of a page and printed at the same height on another page, with the same text (a header or footer) or with only its numbers changed (a page number). A later page that opens with the line the first page opens with ("Jordan Ellery - Resume" over the name, on a document whose first page has no header) opens with its header, which is not read either. Left between the pages, they parted a role's title from its dates: "Senior Engineer, Hooli" at the foot of page 1 and its dates at the top of page 2 were read as "Jordan Ellery" at "Resume, jordan.ellery@example.com".
- **A comma inside a job title** is kept: "Director, Product Management | Acme Corp" was read as "Director" at "Product Management, Acme Corp", and "VP, Engineering, Acme Corp" as "VP" at "Engineering, Acme Corp"; each is now the whole title at Acme Corp. The title keeps its comma when a separator stronger than a comma (a bar, a dash, a tab, "at") parts it from a part naming an organisation, or, with commas only, when a later part names one. An organisation is named by a school's word or by the new `resumeParse.organisationWords` (a company's legal form, "Inc", "LLC", "Ltd", "GmbH", "Corp", "Co"; or an institution's kind, "Hospital", "Clinic", "Bank", "Foundation", "Ministry"), which locale packs can extend. Without one, "Software Engineer, Acme | Berlin, Germany" stays a title, an employer and where. The policy schema gains a field, and the policy's fingerprint changes.
- **An employer written before a title** the policy did not know is read the right way round: "Deloitte, Accountant", "Mercy Hospital, Registered Nurse", "Lincoln High School, Teacher", "Acme Corp, CTO" and "Target - Cashier" were read with the employer as the title. The community policy's `titleWords` gain the titles of work beyond the office (nurse, teacher, tutor, instructor, counselor, therapist, pharmacist, physician, accountant, bookkeeper, auditor, attorney, lawyer, paralegal, cashier, teller, clerk, receptionist, secretary, barista, mechanic, electrician, programmer, recruiter, writer, editor, representative, executive, VP, SVP, EVP, CEO, CFO, CTO, COO, CIO), and with no title word on either side, a first part naming an organisation (`organisationWords` or a school's word: "Globex Corporation, Croupier") is the employer. A title word in the employer's name no longer wins over the title's own: "Account Executive, Head & Shoulders Media" is the account executive at Head & Shoulders Media. The policy's fingerprint changes, and these words count wherever title words do: a line holding one is not taken as the name, nor as a heading the policy does not know.
- **Where a role is worked, in brackets after the employer**, is not part of the employer: "Data Scientist, Netflix (Remote)" was read at "Netflix (Remote)", and is now at Netflix, as "Acme Corp (Austin, TX)" is at Acme Corp. A bracket that is not a place stays: "Acme (Europe)".
