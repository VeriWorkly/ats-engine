# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

Resumes are personal documents. Never post anyone's real resume, including your own, in an issue or a test: replace every personal detail with invented ones first.

## Reporting

Email **grievance@veriworkly.com** about any behaviour that breaks this code. Reports are read by the maintainers, handled in confidence, and answered within a few days. The maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block people who do not follow it, as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
